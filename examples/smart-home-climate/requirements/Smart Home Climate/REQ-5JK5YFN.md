The main PCB shall encrypt firmware images with an accuracy of ±0.5 °C.

The main PCB shall switch schedule entries in under 3 s.

The main PCB shall display access tokens after a power loss.

The main PCB shall log access tokens with an accuracy of ±0.5 °C.

The main PCB shall log user preferences within 200 ms.

The main PCB shall calibrate safety limits in all supported languages.
